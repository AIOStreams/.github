<h1 align="center">AIOStreams</h1>

<p align="center">
  AIOStreams brings your Stremio addons, debrid services and Usenet together into one list, filtered, sorted
  and formatted your way, and plays it in Stremio or in its own apps for every screen.
</p>

The project itself lives at [Viren070/AIOStreams](https://github.com/Viren070/AIOStreams). This
organisation holds the pieces other projects can use on their own:

| Repository | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [libs](https://github.com/AIOStreams/libs) | libmpv, FFmpeg and libass, built for the apps on Android, Windows and macOS |
| [media](https://github.com/AIOStreams/media) | Rust crates for seek-bar thumbnails and subtitle timelines of videos streamed over HTTP |
| [fsmux](https://github.com/AIOStreams/fsmux) | Serve any filesystem over NFSv4, WebDAV and FUSE ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@aiostreams/fsmux)) |
| [seanime-extensions](https://github.com/AIOStreams/seanime-extensions) | A Seanime plugin, torrent provider and custom source for AIOStreams ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@aiostreams/seanime-extensions)) |
| [mpv](https://github.com/AIOStreams/mpv), [FFmpeg](https://github.com/AIOStreams/FFmpeg), [libass](https://github.com/AIOStreams/libass) | Our forks: upstream plus our patches, on each repository's `aiostreams` branch |
